# Patrick Ntwali Matabaro

### Demography · Quantitative Research · Data Analysis

This portfolio brings together selected experiences in applied data analysis and my research in demography and public health, including migration, fertility and reproductive health. It presents my methods, scripts and analytical outputs developed with R and Stata.

**What these projects demonstrate:** preparing data, assessing data quality and validating records, automating tasks, building indicators, conducting statistical analyses and modelling, and communicating findings through maps, reports and dashboards.

## Explore the projects

|[Fertility, education and religion - DRC DHS](projets/01-fecondite-rdc-donnees-denquetes-dhs-rdc/)|Education, community religious context and recent fertility|Stata, R, statistical modelling, multilevel Poisson regression, mapping|
|[Internal migration - REMUAO](projets/02-migrations-remuao/)|Education and residential trajectories in Côte d’Ivoire|Stata, Kaplan–Meier, Cox regression, Fine–Gray models|

## Where to start

* **Demography and quantitative research:** start with the DRC fertility project and the REMUAO event-history analysis, then explore ABIMS.
* **Data analysis and automation:** explore the WMFD record-linkage workflow, ABIMS data-quality analysis and the energy dashboard.
* **Public health:** explore the Madagascar antenatal care project and the comparison of kidney-function measurement methods.

Each project follows the same structure: overview, methods and workflow, available files, and data and reproducibility notes. Projects with supplied figures also include previews and English captions; the larger collections have a complete gallery.

## Data, reproducibility and attribution

Source datasets are not distributed. Some projects provide curated demonstration scripts, while others retain exploratory research code. Running them may require authorised data, intermediate objects and additional preparation. The supplied figures are original outputs, not results recalculated for this portfolio.

ABIMS is a collective project. My contribution covers the scripts I developed, visualisations, data-quality work and participation in methodological discussions; it does not imply authorship of the entire project or the original Shiny application. Internship reports are not included.
